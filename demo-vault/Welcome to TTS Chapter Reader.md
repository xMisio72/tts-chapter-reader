This note shows what the plugin does. Click the **Read note aloud** icon in the left ribbon and listen.

## Every heading is a chapter

The plugin splits a note at its headings. Each heading becomes a chapter in the player, so you can jump straight to the part you want, just like in an audiobook.

- Click a chapter to play it.
- Use the star to mark a favorite.
- Use the eye to skip a chapter you do not need.

## It only records what changed

The first time you listen, the plugin records each chapter and keeps the audio. The next time, playback starts at once.

Change the text of one chapter and only that chapter is recorded again. A yellow dot shows which chapters are new or changed, a green dot which ones are already recorded.

## Listen faster

Drag the speed slider up to 3x. The voice keeps its natural pitch, so it stays easy to follow.

## Your notes stay on your computer

The built-in voice runs on your own machine. Nothing you write is sent anywhere, and there is no account and no API key.
